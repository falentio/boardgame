## Video direction

- **Palette** (from frame.md, broadside): dark register is the video's ground (ink-black #111111, cream #F0ECE5 type, 1px hairline rules border-dark). Fire-orange #E85D26 is the ONLY accent and is rationed to four meanings: coin costs, a life lost (face-up reveal), challenge outcomes, and the forced-Coup stamp. Frame 9's URL card switches to the orange register (fire-orange ground, ink-black type). No third surface, no shadows, sharp corners, no rounded corners.
- **Motion grammar**: long-tail `power3`-class eases, smooth over bouncy; every frame follows the VO-paced reveal model — a piece enters when the voiceover names it, never before; reveals spread across the back ~50%. Holds are real stillness (no lazy breathing, no forced drift; subtle jitter only where named). Both failure modes banned: the slideshow (front-load then freeze) and the screensaver (elements floating independently).
- **Rhythm / held beats**: Frames 1, 5, and 8 are the percussive beats; Frames 4 and 9 carry the deliberate breather holds (table settle, end card); Frames 3, 6, 7 build steadily. No frame is uniformly busy, no frame dumps at t=0.
- **Caption band**: captions occupy the bottom ~17%; all content plans into the top ~83%, centered heroes anchor near y≈0.42.
- **Negative list**: purple-blue "AI" gradients, floating bokeh, generic decorative shapes, real cursors, browser chrome, rounded corners, drop shadows, and any prop that could belong to another product's video unchanged.
- **Recurring invented vocabulary** (the film's props): the influence card (tall card, cream back with mono label, flips to a colored role face), the coin (disc with count-up), the role card (public row card), the Court deck (15-stack), the claim chip (mono pill).
