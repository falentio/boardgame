## Intent

A how-to-play explainer for Coup: Rebellion G54 (2014, Rikki Tahta) — the standalone
bluffing and deduction game, not an expansion. The viewer should finish able to sit
down and run game one: what the game is, the variable 5-role setup, the turn, claims
and challenges, blocks, losing influence, and how a game ends. Tone: a clever friend
teaching at the table — the game is political backstabbing, so dry wit is welcome,
but every rule on screen must be exact. One watch, no homework.

## Notes

- Source of truth: ./docs/research/coup-rebellion-g54/*.md in this repo (the rules
  research the web implementation is built on), plus docs/design/g54-full-game.md.
- Key facts the video must not get wrong: standalone game; 3–6 players; ~15 min;
  roles per game = 1 Finance + 1 Communications + 1 Force + 2 Special Interest out
  of 25 in the box; influence deck is 15 cards (3 copies × 5 roles); 2 coins and 2
  face-down influence cards each; Income +1; Coup costs 7, always works, unblockable;
  10+ coins at turn start forces Coup; challenge loser loses a life, winner shuffles
  back and redraws from the Court; only the target may block by default; Force roles
  block themselves; loser picks which card to reveal; doubled life loss in one turn
  is legal; last player holding a face-down card wins, no second place.
