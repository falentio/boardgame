# 03 Turn Structure and Rules

## Base Rules Hold

Anarchy uses the base G54 turn structure with no changes. One action per turn. No passing. Claim, challenge, block, and resolution follow the base order. See `docs/research/coup-rebellion-g54/03-turn-structure-and-rules.md` for the full base sequence.

The 10 coin forced Coup rule is unchanged. The 7 coin Coup is unchanged. Hidden hand rules are unchanged.

## General Actions

Always available. No influence needed. Cannot be challenged. Cannot be blocked.

Income is take 1 coin from Treasury. Income is replaced by Bank when Financier is in play.
Coup is pay 7 coins to Treasury and choose a target. Target loses 1 influence. Always succeeds.
Bank is take 1 coin from Treasury and add 1 coin to the Bank pile. Only in play with Financier.
Social Media is take 1 card from the deck and return 1 card to the deck. Only in play if selected.

## Role Actions

Anarchy adds six role actions. Each is claimed like a base role action. Claim does not require proof unless challenged.

Anarchist is special. The action may be taken without holding the Anarchist card. The claim itself cannot be challenged because there is no held card to prove. The pass and defuse steps are counteractions and may be challenged.

## The Bomb Sub Turn

Anarchist opens a pass loop that runs outside normal turn order.

1. Active player pays 3 coins to Treasury and takes the Bomb token.
2. Active player names a target. The target may not be a prior holder, including the active player.
3. The target passes the bomb or defuses it by claiming Anarchist. Each claim may be challenged.
4. If the target passes, the new holder repeats step 3. The passing player joins the prior holder list.
5. If the target defuses, the turn ends and the bomb returns to the center.
6. If the target does not pass and does not defuse, the target loses 1 influence and the bomb returns to the center.

The bomb may never return to a prior holder. The prior holder list grows with each pass. The loop ends only on a defuse or an influence loss.

## Sub Turns Added by Other Roles

Socialist opens a collection loop for every other player. Each target gives 1 coin or 1 card. Then the active player reshuffles cards and deals hidden replacements. This runs inside the active player's turn.

Plantation Owner opens a mass claim. Every other player may claim the role. Each claim may be challenged. Payout runs after all claims resolve.

Arms Dealer reveals 2 deck cards to the table, checks them against the named role, then reshuffles.

## Challenges and Counters

Unchanged from base G54. Any role action or counteraction may be challenged. Any player may challenge. General actions may never be challenged.

A block stops the loss or theft but does not refund attacker costs. The Bomb pass is a counteraction, so it follows the same rule. The 3 coins paid for the Anarchist action stay paid even if the bomb is defused.

## Losing Influence and Elimination

Unchanged from base G54. Face down cards are lives. The loser chooses which card to reveal. Zero face down cards means elimination. The double life loss rule still applies.

The Bomb can cause a life loss outside a normal attack. It resolves at the end of the pass loop.
