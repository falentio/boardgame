# 02 Setup and Components

## Box Contents

18 influence cards. Three copies of each of the 6 new roles.
7 role action cards. One for each of the 6 new roles, plus one Social Media.
1 general action card. Bank.
1 role token. Bomb.
6 role dividers. One per new role.
Rulebook.

One source lists the 7 role action cards as 6 roles plus one general action and does not name the Bank card. The rulebook lists the Bank card on its own. Treat Bank as its own general action card.

## Table Setup Steps

1. Separate the base G54 role action cards into the 4 piles by type.
2. Add the 7 Anarchy role action cards to the matching piles. Finance gains Financier and Plantation Owner. Force gains Anarchist and Paramilitary. Special Interest gains Arms Dealer and Socialist. Communications gains Social Media.
3. Choose 5 role action cards. Take 1 Finance, 1 Communications, 1 Force, 2 Special Interest. Random or by vote.
4. If Social Media is among the chosen cards, move it to the general action area and draw one extra Communications role. Social Media is now an extra general action.
5. If Financier is among the chosen cards, swap the Income general action card for the Bank general action card.
6. Place the chosen role cards face up and visible to all.
7. Place the Bomb token in the center if Anarchist is in the game. It is the only new token.
8. Pull 3 copies of each chosen role for the influence deck. Shuffle. Deal 2 face down to each player.
9. Give each player 2 coins. Coins stay visible. Remainder is the Treasury.
10. Last winner starts. Turns go clockwise.

## Social Media Setup Detail

Social Media is a role action card that plays as a general action. When it is selected, it leaves the role area and the table draws one additional Communications role. The game still needs a Communications role. Without the extra draw the game would have only 4 roles and no card swap role.

## Financier Setup Detail

Bank replaces Income only when Financier is in play. Place the Bank card where Income normally sits. The Bank pile starts empty. Every Bank action adds 1 coin to it. Financier empties it.

## Public Versus Hidden State

Public is active role list, all coin counts, revealed influence cards, Court deck size, Treasury size, Bank pile size, Bomb holder, Bomb prior holder list, token locations, whose turn it is, pending claim and target.

Hidden is each player's face down influence. Never reveal to other players except through challenge proof or loss reveal. Socialist card collection and redistribution stay hidden. Arms Dealer reveal is public. Web version must enforce hidden hands server side.

## Coin and Deck Limits

No coin cap per player except forced Coup at 10. Treasury has no cap. Bank pile has no cap. If coins run out use counters.

Court deck size is 15 minus 2 per player at start. Arms Dealer turns over 2 deck cards and reshuffles them, so deck size is unchanged. Socialist draws 1 from the deck and returns 1, so deck size is unchanged. Deck can still empty with base draw roles. Rules allow partial actions when cards or coins are short. Web version must handle an empty deck without crash.
