# Sources

All claims in this folder trace to fetched pages below. Search snippets alone were never used as evidence for role text. Role text core is the official Anarchy rulebook cross checked with UltraBoardGames rules and a full card review.

## Official Rules and Publisher Pages

https://www.spelhuis.be/Files/7/112000/112353/Attachments/Product/5v83Y8078c0p39y2d24t47U95v35870v.pdf
Official Anarchy rulebook page. Contents list, setup rule, Social Media extra Communications draw rule.

https://www.ultraboardgames.com/coup/anarchy.php
Full Anarchy rules. Components, setup, role list, Anarchist, Paramilitary, Plantation Owner, Financier, Arms Dealer, Socialist, Social Media action text.

https://indieboardsandcards.com/our-games/coup-rebellion-g54-anarchy/
Publisher page. Release 2016, designer Rikki Tahta, illustrator Andrew Higgins, MSRP, role count framing.

https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/ibcgames/g54-anarchy
Kickstarter page. Six roles plus one general action, role names, promo contents, G54 required to play.

https://sites.google.com/view/la-mame-games/g54---anarchy
La Mame Games page for the Guatemala 1954 twin edition. Freedom of Press general action, World Bank loan card, Anarchist bomb.
