# 06 Variants and Player Counts

## Player count is the only variant axis

Player count is the only variant axis the rules define [RB, RP].

No official variants, expansions, team variants, or alternate win conditions exist in any source read [RB, RP, SB]. There is no variant that changes the two-role structure. SPACE-BIFF! notes that the game needs no wacky role cards and no alternate ways to win [SB].

## The three setup differences by count

Setup differs by count in exactly three ways [RB, RP].

Which player mats are used. Each mat is marked with its player counts in its top right corner, and setup takes the mats whose marker matches the number of players [RB, RP].

Token placement. Three and four players get one placement pass. Five and six players get a second pass, so two locations carry two tokens [RB, RP].

Which evidence cards are used. The deck is filtered by the bottom-right marker on each card, and the filter therefore also fixes how many cards each player holds, which is two or three [RB, RP].

The card mix differs per player count, and two independent sources confirm it. SPACE-BIFF! says each player count has its own card mix [SB]. Dale Yu says the separate sets of player cards exist because the charts have to work with each other to make sure there is only one scapegoat for each particular die roll [DY].

## The six-player-only rule

A player may go to the cops at the beginning of the turn of the player sitting three seats to their left [RB, RP].

This is the only count-specific rule beyond setup, and it exists only at six players [RB, RP]. `04-frame-attempt-and-end.md` covers the timing.

## Token placement by count

With 3 players the tokens land on Prepare, Spy, and Trade [RB, RP].

With 4 players the tokens land on Prepare, Spy, Trade, and Stash [RB, RP].

With 5 or 6 players a second pass gives two locations two tokens each and the rest one [RB, RP].

No player token begins on Go to the Cops at any count [RB]. After setup, at most two location cards may carry two player tokens [RB].

## What does not vary

The three-part turn does not vary by count. Every turn is movement, action, and Evidence Swap in that order [RB, RP].

The two end conditions do not vary by count. The game ends when a frame attempt succeeds or when a player goes to the cops [RB, RP].

The going-to-the-cops outcome does not vary by count. The scapegoat wins whoever runs, at every count [RB, RP, RD, GNG, JTR].

The wrong-player frame outcome does not vary by count. Framing a player who is not the scapegoat hands the win to the scapegoat at every count [RB, RP, RD, GNG, JTR].

The two decoder dice and the one-time roll do not vary by count. The same two numbers are read for the rest of the game at every count [RB, RP].

Open communication does not vary by count. Players may talk freely and may lie about their cards at every count [RB, RP, DY, SB].

The frame procedure does not vary by count. Every player picks a card, and all reveal on a count of three, at every count [RB, RP].

## Unofficial play patterns

Reviewers note that the game is quiet and discourages open table talk compared to louder social-deduction games [RD]. Reviewers also note that it works best with repeated plays by the same group [RD]. These are play-pattern observations and not variants [RD].
