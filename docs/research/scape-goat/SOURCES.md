# Sources

All claims in this folder trace to the fetched pages below. The rulebook is the primary source, and the rulespal transcript mirrors it verbatim. BoardGameGeek was unreachable on every fetch, so the facts that rest on it appear with the marker `BGG-via-search` and never as a direct read.

## Primary rules

https://www.jonperry.com/scapegoat-rules.pdf
Official rulebook, Scape Goat, Indie Boards and Cards, 8 pages, hosted by the designer. Read 2026-08-21. Internal filename `Scape Goat rulebook 02 03 2020.indd`, dated 2/17/2020. Source of the component counts, the setup order, the turn structure, the location actions, the frame procedure, the two end conditions, the communication rules, and the worked example. Tagged [RB].

https://rulespal.com/scape-goat/rulebook
Rulespal transcript of the rulebook. Read 2026-08-21. Full text mirror of the rulebook, used to confirm the rulebook wording. Tagged [RP].

## Designer and publisher

https://www.jonperry.com/scapegoat.html
Designer's page for the game. Read 2026-08-21. Marketing blurb and the player count. No rules. Tagged [JP].

## Reviews and play reports

https://spacebiff.com/2020/10/07/scape-goat/
Dan Thurot, "The Goat Will Carry On", SPACE-BIFF!. Published 2020-10-07, read 2026-08-21. The only source describing the decoder matrix as a grid of over a hundred options per player mat, and the only source confirming that a die roll maps to the same scapegoat for a given player count. Also the source for the two-role structure and the two player mat properties. Tagged [SB].

https://opinionatedgamers.com/2021/02/03/dale-yu-first-impressions-of-scape-goat/
Dale Yu, "First Impressions of Scape Goat", The Opinionated Gamers. Published 2021-02-03, read 2026-08-21. Source of the age 14 plus rating, the d10 and d12 dice, the 10 by 12 grid, the 30 minute time, and the per-count card mix note. Tagged [DY].

https://gamesnightguru.com/game/scape-goat/
"Scape Goat", Games Night Guru. Published 2020-08-29, read 2026-08-20. States that players begin with two cards and names four locations. Also confirms the wrong-player-framed outcome. Tagged [GNG].

https://tabletopia.com/games/scape-goat
Tabletopia listing. Read 2026-08-21. Credits the designer, artist, and publisher, and gives age 10 plus, 3 to 6 players, 30 minutes, and weight 6.9. Tagged [TT].

https://www.reddit.com/r/boardgames/comments/uis1xj/impressions_of_scape_goat_an_innovative_and_fun/
r/boardgames impressions thread (EndersGame_Reviewer). Read 2026-08-21. About five years old at read time (2021-2022). Confirms the wrong-player-goes-to-the-cops outcome and reports a 15 to 20 minute session. Tagged [RD].

https://www.jestatharogue.com/tabletop-games/scape-goat-card-game-first-impressions/
Jesta ThaRogue, "Scape Goat Card Game First Impressions". Read 2026-08-21. States that a player has two or three actions to choose from, and notes glass-bead preparation tokens. Tagged [JTR].

## Facts that rest on search snippets

BoardGameGeek was unreachable, so three facts rest on BGG search-result snippets rather than a fetched page. They carry the marker `BGG-via-search` in the files that state them.

- The release year tag "(2020)", which the rulebook's own dated file and the designer's page also support [RB, JP].
- The community player-count recommendation "Best: 4-5". This fact rests on the snippet alone, and Dale Yu's note that the game improves at higher counts is the only other support [DY].
- The 30 minute playing time, which Tabletopia and Dale Yu also state directly [TT, DY].

Every rule, count, and procedure in this folder traces to the rulebook or to a fetched review.
