# 08 Tokens and Special Cases

## Token Inventory

Treaty times 2. Peacekeeping times 1. Disappear times 3. Tax times 2. Total 8. Tokens enter play only when their role is active. Unused tokens stay in box.

## Treaty Foreign Consular

Two tokens. Holders are allies. Cannot target each other by any targeted action, including Coup. Lasts until they are the final two players, then expires. New Foreign Consular claim reassigns treaties. Old treaty is discarded. Web state is ally pair IDs plus expiry check at 2 players.

Target picker must exclude ally for all attacks, steals, Producer takes, treaty gives, Communist steal. Challenge is still allowed against ally. Priest table payment to ally is unresolved. Treat as allowed because Priest has no target selection. List as open question.

## Peacekeeping Peacekeeper

One token. Holder is immune to targeting except by Coup. Challenges still allowed. New claim steals token. Immediate loss of protection for prior holder. Web state is holder ID or none. Target picker excludes holder for all non Coup targeting.

## Disappear Mercenary

Three tokens. Placed on target at claim time. Target loses 1 influence after their next turn. Target gets one full turn first. Block only at placement. Resolution is automatic unless target already eliminated. Web state is target ID plus turns remaining. Show countdown badge. Trigger Missionary and Intellectual windows on resolution.

## Tax Customs Officer

Two tokens. One stays with holder. One marks a role card. Every claim of the marked role by another player costs 1 extra paid to the holder. Applies per claim. New Customs Officer claim moves the mark. Web state is marked role ID plus holder ID. Auto charge before challenge window. Show tax icon on role board.

## Special Timing Cases

Capitalist secondary collection resolves clockwise and each collection is challengeable.
Priest multi payment resolves clockwise with per payer block.
General multi block resolves per target clockwise with separate challenges.
Protestor funding window is open to all non targets, then target block.
Lawyer window opens on elimination before Treasury return.
Missionary and Intellectual windows open after influence loss, including loss from challenge, Force, Coup for Intellectual only, Mercenary resolution, General.
Spy chained action opens a second full action with its own windows.
Crime Boss pay or suffer choice is a target decision, not a block claim.

## Partial Actions

Allowed when coins or cards are short. Take what is available. Web version must never block a legal claim only because Treasury or Court is short. Show partial badge in log.

## Double Loss

Legal in three patterns. Failed attack challenge plus execution. False block plus execution. Intellectual or Missionary failed defense plus extra loss. UI must support selecting two cards in one sequence and must not end turn early after first reveal.

## Hidden Information Rules

Never reveal hidden hands. Never allow card peeking. Challenge proof shows one card to all, then shuffles it away and draws hidden replacement. Communications draws go only to actor. Producer target choice is hidden except counts. Treaty and tax and coin counts are public.
