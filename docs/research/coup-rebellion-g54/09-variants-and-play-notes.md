# 09 Variants and Play Notes

## Advanced Roles

Rules say include advanced roles only when all players are experienced. Box does not mark which roles are advanced with a single authoritative list across sources. Starter set is safe. Web version should tag complex roles as advanced in UI. Suggested advanced list is Speculator, Spy, Writer, Newscaster, Producer, General, Mercenary, Crime Boss, Customs Officer, Communist, Missionary, Intellectual, Lawyer, Protestor. Suggested basic list is Banker, Farmer, Capitalist, Director, Reporter, Guerrilla, Judge, Politician, Priest, Peacekeeper, Foreign Consular. Treat this split as product judgment, not official canon. Confirm with physical role cards when available.

## Player Count Variants

Standard is 3 to 6. Seven to 8 is possible with 4 copies per role from Kickstarter. Web version should support 3 to 6 at launch. Allow 7 to 8 as custom room option only if deck is 4 copies per role. Note that Missionary with 6 players is rated poor. Intellectual is preferred at high counts. Writer and Newscaster deck dig reduces bluffing at 6. Spy plus Customs Officer is harsh. Crime Boss plus Politician overlap is dull.

## Draft Variants Seen in Play

Random by category is official. Vote draft is common. Fixed set all night builds meta and helps new players. Swap one role per game introduces variety gradually. Six roles spicy mode adds one extra role. Team Reformation variant from base Coup is sometimes added but is out of scope for G54 web version.

## Balance Notes From Community

G54 is less tightly balanced than base Coup. Some combinations stall on defense. Judge plus Protestor plus treaty sets can freeze aggression. Producer cycling can dominate. Capitalist encourages lying and speeds income. Customs Officer creates a sub meta around taxing the strongest role. Peacekeeper alone does little without other attacks in the set. General is swingy with many simultaneous blocks. Protestor often underperforms because funding is unreliable and only saves 2 over Coup. Keep these notes for recommended sets, not hard rules.
