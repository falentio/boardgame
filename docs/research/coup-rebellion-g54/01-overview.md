# 01 Overview

## Identity

Name is Coup Rebellion G54. Short name is G54. Designer is Rikki Tahta. Publishers are La Mame Games and Indie Boards and Cards. Year is 2014. It is a standalone game. It is not an expansion. Ownership of base Coup is not required. Art is political dystopia themed.

It derives from Coup Guatemala 1954. Rules are substantively the same as that title with new art.

## Player Count and Session

Standard is 3 to 6 players. Best with 4 to 6. Two player is not supported by design. About one third of roles degrade with two players. Kickstarter edition shipped 4 copies per role and was played with 7 to 8. Retail box ships 3 copies per role. Session is about 15 minutes with a known role set. First sessions with new roles run 30 to 45 minutes. Age rating is 14 plus. Difficulty is easy to learn.

## Win Condition

Last player with influence wins. Influence means at least one face down influence card. There is no scoring. There is no second place. When elimination leaves one player the game ends at once.

## Core Loop

Each turn the active player selects exactly one action. Action is either general or role claimed. General actions are Income and Coup. Role actions come from the 5 active role cards. Claim does not require proof unless challenged.

Other players may challenge the claim. Any player may challenge. Challenge is resolved first. Loser loses one influence. Winner of a truthful challenge shuffles the shown card into the Court and draws a replacement.

Some actions have targets. Target may claim a counteraction. Counteraction may also be challenged. Unchallenged counter succeeds. Successful counter prevents the loss or theft but does not refund attacker costs unless the role says so.

## Deck Variability

Box has 25 roles. Game uses 5. Selection rule is 1 Finance plus 1 Communications plus 1 Force plus 2 Special Interest. Selection may be random or drafted. Starter set is Banker plus Director plus Guerrilla plus Politician plus Peacekeeper.

Total combinations are 5 times 5 times 5 times 45. The 45 is 10 choose 2. Result is 5625. Each combination plays differently. Income rich sets play fast. Defensive sets play slow. Attack heavy sets need Peacekeeper or treaty roles to stay balanced.

## Relation to Base Coup

Engine is shared. Bluff, challenge, block, Income, Coup, 10 coin forced Coup, 7 coin Coup. Role set is not compatible one to one. Base Coup has cross role answers such as Contessa blocks Assassin and Ambassador blocks steal. G54 has no guaranteed cross answers because the active set varies. Each Force role is blocked by itself. Finance and Communications roles mostly have no block.

G54 box cannot recreate base Coup exactly. It can approximate it with the starter set. Banker acts as Duke. Guerrilla acts as Assassin and Contessa in one. Director acts as Ambassador. Politician acts as Captain.

## Implementation Scope

Needs public revealed cards. Needs Court deck count. Needs turn timer and forced Coup enforcement. Needs challenge window and block window on every claim. Needs target picker with treaty and Peacekeeper filtering. Needs reactive windows for Missionary, Intellectual, Lawyer, Protestor third party payment, Capitalist shared payout, Communist poorest recipient selection.
